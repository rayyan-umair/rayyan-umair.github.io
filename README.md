## Who I am

I'm 21. I didn't get here through a straight line.

Growing up, I spent years looking at the world through three different lenses: medicine, engineering, and eventually technology. Nobody handed me that exposure. I went looking for it myself, out of pure curiosity, because I couldn't help but want to understand how things worked and, more importantly, how they broke.

AI and ML pulled me into IT first. Then I started asking the obvious follow-up question: **what happens when the model is wrong, and who owns that call?** That question led me directly to security. Not as a detour, but as the natural place where AI and accountability collide.

Two years of real support and security operations work later, I've confirmed something about myself: I don't want to stay at the task level. I think in systems. I get restless fixing the same class of problem twice when the architecture is what needs changing.

> *"Technology evolves quickly. Responsibility does not."*

◈  Security Architecture & Risk

     I want to sit exactly at that boundary and make sure it holds.

◈  Human Accountability in Systems
     Someone has to walk into the boardroom when things go wrong and be trusted, not just credentialed.
     That's the role I'm building toward.
